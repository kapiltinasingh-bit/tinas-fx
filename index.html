How the desk works

Research runs

Twice every weekday, before the London session (07:48 Dubai time) and before New York (15:48), the desk reads the latest gold coverage from several outlets, central-bank and ETF data, and the economic calendar. It then writes a brief, updates the levels and the desk call, and logs every source it used.

The desk call

Each brief ends in one of three calls. BUY or SELL means price has confirmed a scenario. WAIT means price is between the trigger levels and the desk is standing aside. Each scenario names its trigger, its stop and two targets, so the risk is known before any trade.

Levels

Support and resistance come from classic daily pivots, the 20- and 200-day averages, round numbers that many traders watch, and levels that several independent sources agree on.

The record

Every call is logged with its outcome, wins and losses alike. Nothing is deleted after the fact.

Limits

  • The spot price refreshes every minute from gold-api.com and the chart streams from TradingView. Levels and the desk call refresh with each research run. Always confirm with your broker’s feed before acting.
  • Gold reacts sharply to US inflation data, Fed decisions and geopolitics. Levels can break without warning.
  • This is educational research. It is not personal financial advice and does not know your situation.

When the market gets tough, we get tougher.

How the desk works

Research runs

Twice every weekday, before the London session (07:48 Dubai time) and before New York (15:48), the desk reads the latest coverage for every desk market from several outlets, central-bank and economic data, and the calendar. It writes a brief, updates each market’s levels and call, and logs every source.

The desk call

Each desk market carries one of three calls. BUY or SELL means price has confirmed a scenario. WAIT means price sits between the trigger levels. Scenarios name the trigger and targets, and a stop where the levels support one.

Levels

Support and resistance come from daily pivots, moving averages, round numbers and levels several independent sources agree on.

Live data

Prices, charts, technical ratings, heatmaps, screeners, the calendar and live headlines stream from TradingView. Gold, silver and Bitcoin also refresh every minute from gold-api.com. Desk levels and calls update with each research run.

The record

Every call is logged with its outcome, wins and losses alike. Nothing is deleted after the fact.

Limits

  • Markets react sharply to inflation data, central-bank decisions and geopolitics. Levels can break without warning.
  • Some exchanges restrict free embeds; where a widget can’t load, use the TradingView link.
  • This is educational research. It is not personal financial advice and does not know your situation.
A letter from the founder
Mushkil waqt, commando sakht.
Tough times call for tougher discipline.

Markets don’t care how we feel. Gold can move a hundred dollars in an afternoon on one inflation number, a currency can turn on a single speech, and in those moments the only thing that protects you is discipline.

I started Tina’s FX because I wanted one honest place to read the markets every morning: the price, the levels that matter, the news from many sources, and a clear plan, with every source linked so you can check it yourself.

Here, every call shows its reasons, and every loss stays on the record. We don’t promise winners. We promise preparation: know your levels, size your risk, and never trade money you can’t afford to lose.

When the market gets tough, we get tougher.

Tina Singh
Founder, Tina’s FX

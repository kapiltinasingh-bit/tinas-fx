    How to take a signal

    1. Wait for the window. Setups are only valid inside the session times shown. Outside them, do nothing.
    2. Wait for the sweep. Price must first run through the liquidity level (the stops beyond the range edge).
    3. Wait for confirmation. A 5-minute candle closes back inside the range and the 5m structure flips (change of character).
    4. Enter in the zone with a limit order, using the lot size for your account. Place the stop at once.
    5. Manage the trade. At target 1, close half and move the stop to entry. If the cancel condition prints before entry, the setup is void.

    Lot sizes assume standard contracts (gold 100 oz, silver 5,000 oz, oil 1,000 barrels, forex 100,000 units). Check your broker’s contract size and minimum lot (usually 0.01). Signals are educational research, not personal advice. They can and do fail: see the track record and risk notice. For any other trade use the position size calculator.

    R is the result measured in units of risk: a trade that risked 1 and made 2 is +2R; a stopped trade is −1R. WAIT calls set the triggers to watch and score 0 unless a trigger fills. “Proof” opens the public GitHub update that first contained the call. The full history of every change is here.

    How calls are graded

    • A call is tracked live by the signal watcher, which checks one-minute prices about once a minute and logs the moment a call fills, hits a target or is stopped. The result goes on the record at the next research update.
    • Hit target 1 before the stop: win, scored at the planned reward to risk.
    • Hit the stop first: loss, scored −1R.
    • Replaced by a new plan with neither level touched: expired, scored 0.
    • Results ignore spreads, commissions and slippage, which would lower real results.

    Last updated 18 October 2026

    • Crypto is extremely volatile, trades every day, and can be affected by exchange failures and regulation.

    How the desk works

    Research runs

    Four times every weekday, at 06:20 Dubai time (before India opens), 10:20 (before London), 15:20 (before New York) and 20:20 (New York session), the desk reads the latest coverage for every desk market from several outlets, central-bank and economic data, and the calendar. It writes a brief, updates each market’s levels and call, and logs every source.

    The desk call

    Each desk market carries one of three calls. BUY or SELL means price has confirmed a scenario. WAIT means price sits between the trigger levels. Scenarios name the trigger and targets, and a stop where the levels support one.

    Levels

    Support and resistance come from daily pivots, moving averages, round numbers and levels several independent sources agree on.

    Live data

    Prices, charts, technical ratings, heatmaps, screeners, the calendar and live headlines stream from TradingView. Gold, silver and Bitcoin also refresh every minute from gold-api.com, and the signal watcher updates prices for every desk market and the status of every signal about once a minute. Desk levels and calls update with each research run, four times each weekday.

    The record

    Every call is logged with its outcome, wins and losses alike. Nothing is deleted after the fact.

    Limits

    • Markets react sharply to inflation data, central-bank decisions and geopolitics. Levels can break without warning.
    • Some exchanges restrict free embeds; where a widget can’t load, use the TradingView link.
